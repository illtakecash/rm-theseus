# Seal log

Publication seal for the ferry fixture. Records were appended together when the example was frozen, not at each in-world act. The header is not part of the hash chain. Each record is one line. `prev` is `genesis` on the first record. Every later `prev` is the SHA-256 of the previous record line including its terminating newline. `body` is the SHA-256 of that file's exact bytes. This is publication revision 2. It re-seals every record after a pre-disclosure review completed the act manifests, and it supersedes the revision 1 log sealed in commit `80b085bf84f158da317444281520e39638e7b887`; that log's digests are no longer the example. Paths are rooted at `experiments/human-optimized/`.
